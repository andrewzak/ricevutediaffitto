Ricevute di Affitto

The free Android app that fills in, calculates and prints rent receipts. Three receipts per A4 sheet, ready to sign, and up to 12 receipts in a single PDF to close the year in a minute.

Please note: the app is currently in Italian and creates receipts in the Italian format, designed for properties rented in Italy.

What it does

Automatic calculations

Rent, ISTAT adjustment (Italian inflation index), extra charges, condominium fees, VAT and revenue stamp: the total and the amount in words are filled in automatically.

Up to 12 receipts

Prepare the whole year at once. The next number, date and month are suggested for every receipt.

Fix it anytime

From the receipts list you can edit or delete any receipt before creating the PDF.

Print-ready PDF

Three receipts per A4 sheet, in the classic layout of the Italian paper form, with space for the revenue stamp.

Your data stays yours

No account and no connection: everything stays on your phone and is never sent to anyone.

Free

No subscription. If you find it useful, you can support the project.

Guide

Filling in the form

  • Fill in the receipt fields. No field is mandatory: leave them empty if you want to print blank receipts.
  • In date fields type digits only: 01012026 becomes 01/01/2026. Days, months and years are checked.

Amounts

  • The total is calculated automatically: enter rent, ISTAT, extra charges and condominium fees, plus VAT on the taxable amount, plus the revenue stamp. Enter amounts without the euro symbol, and ISTAT and VAT as plain numbers without the percent sign.
  • The amount in words is written automatically in the PDF (in Italian).

Number, date and month

  • After each receipt is added, the next number, the date (one month later) and the month are suggested.
  • If the month doesn't match the date, a warning appears, but you can still go ahead in special cases.

ip

  • If you prepare all receipts at the end of the year, just create the first receipt starting from January, with its number, issue date and all the other fields, then tap "Aggiungi al PDF" (Add to PDF) repeatedly until you reach 12. The app prepares the receipts increasing the receipt number, the date and the month in sequence, keeping all the other fields unchanged: your receipts are ready to print in less than a minute.

Sheet and PDF

  • Tap "Aggiungi al PDF" (Add to PDF) to put the receipt on the sheet. The sheet holds up to 12 receipts.
  • In the Ricevute (Receipts) screen you can edit or delete each receipt. After editing, tap "Aggiorna ricevuta" (Update receipt).
  • Tap "Crea PDF" (Create PDF) and choose where to save it: each A4 sheet holds 3 receipts.
  • The sheet and the form are saved automatically on your phone: if the app is closed by a phone call or by the system, you'll find everything as you left it.

Reset buttons

  • Svuota campi (Clear fields) resets the form.
  • Resetta date (Reset dates) clears the dates and the number. The contract registration date is kept.
  • Svuota foglio (Clear sheet) deletes all the receipts on the sheet.
Important. The receipts do not replace the registration of the lease contract or the tax return.
